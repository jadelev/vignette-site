How we work

We begin with how you want people to feel: before they arrive, while they’re there, and long after.

  1. i

    Listening

    Who you are, who’s coming, where in the world, and why now.

  2. ii

    Inquiry

    A creative deep dive. We name the feelings, the tenets and what each guest should carry home.

  3. iii

    Architecture

    Budget, venue, vendors, run of show. Rigorous production, invisible to your guests.

  4. iv

    The Gathering

    On site from first light to last song, holding every thread so you can be in it.

  5. v

    Afterglow

    A retrospective together: what landed, what it stirred, and what comes next.

Sourced with care

Beautiful and considered. Every choice is a choice.

Rent before we buy

Wherever it’s possible, we source rentals, vintage and borrowed pieces before anything new is made or bought.

No single-use plastic

We design it out from the start, from the bar to the welcome bags, and hold that line even when it takes more work.

Vendors who walk the walk

We work with local makers, growers and crews whose values match their marketing.

Questions

Frequently asked

What does Vignette do?

Vignette is a luxury event design and production studio. We design and produce weddings, elopements and wedding weeks, immersive dinners, retreats, intimate festivals and brand gatherings, from the first idea to the last song.

Do you plan destination weddings?

Yes. We’re based in Boulder, Colorado and work worldwide, with gatherings produced in more than twenty countries across North America, Latin America, Europe and Asia, and led workshops as far away as Australia.

Can Jade officiate or lead our ceremony?

Yes. Jade designs and holds ceremonies herself, including wedding vows, blessings, baby blessings, women’s circles and threshold rituals, rooted in tradition or written from scratch.

How much does it cost to work with Vignette?

Our full-service studio fee starts at 20% of the total investment in your gathering. Share your plans through the enquiry form and we’ll talk through scope together.

How do you approach sustainability?

We rent before we buy, design out single-use plastic from the start, and work with local makers, growers and crews whose values match their marketing.
